Add `prompt-engineering`, a model-invoked skill that reviews or writes LLM prompts from the text of The Prompt Report (Schulhoff et al., 2025). It inventories every model call in scope, gives a first-pass review with its questions when context is missing, and quotes the paper by section for every finding. The paper's text ships with the skill under CC BY 4.0.
