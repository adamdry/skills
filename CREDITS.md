## The Prompt Report

`skills/engineering/prompt-engineering/paper/` holds the paper, and `skills/engineering/prompt-engineering/references/` holds its text:

Sander Schulhoff, Michael Ilie, Nishant Balepur, Konstantine Kahadze, Amanda Liu, Chenglei Si, Yinheng Li, Aayush Gupta, HyoJung Han, Sevien Schulhoff, Pranav Sandeep Dulepet, Saurav Vidyadhara, Dayeon Ki, Sweta Agrawal, Chau Pham, Gerson Kroiz, Feileen Li, Hudson Tao, Ashay Srivastava, Hevander Da Costa, Saloni Gupta, Megan L. Rogers, Inna Goncearenco, Giuseppe Sarli, Igor Galynker, Denis Peskoff, Marine Carpuat, Jules White, Shyamal Anadkat, Alexander Hoyle and Philip Resnik. *The Prompt Report: A Systematic Survey of Prompt Engineering Techniques*. [arXiv:2406.06608v6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06608v6), 26 February 2025.

The paper is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (CC BY 4.0). Those two folders stay under that licence. The repo's MIT licence does not cover them.

Changes made: the PDF is unchanged. The files in `references/` are extracts of sections 1 to 6 and 8 and Appendix A.2, reflowed from the PDF into Markdown. Headings and bold terms follow the paper, footnotes are marked `[fn N]`, figures are transcribed where they hold text and otherwise pointed to by page, and hyphens lost at line ends were restored. Each file's header says the same.
